Privacy Policy

appGo for macOS · Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

appGo is a macOS desktop assistant that provides local weather, system monitoring and shortcuts for launching your own apps. We designed it to work entirely on your Mac: appGo does not operate a server and does not collect your personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data. appGo has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking. We never sell or share your data.

2. Information stored on your Mac

appGo saves the following settings locally in the macOS preferences of the app. These values stay on your device unless you back them up yourself:

  • the list of app shortcuts you added (application names and paths);
  • whether “Launch at login” is enabled;
  • your interface language choice;
  • the city you manually selected for weather, if any;
  • a cached place name and approximate coordinates, used to speed up weather loading.

Removing these items in the app, or deleting the app and its preferences, removes this data.

3. Location

If you allow location access, appGo requests your approximate location only to show the weather where you are. The coordinates are sent directly to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve the forecast, exactly like the system Weather app. They are not sent to us, because appGo has no server.

You can deny location access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. appGo then falls back to a city you choose manually.

4. Weather data

Weather information is provided by Apple WeatherKit and is subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy. appGo only displays the data returned by Apple; it does not store it on any server.

5. Automation (Apple Events)

The “Quit Shortcuts” feature asks macOS for permission to send a quit request to the applications you added. This permission is used only for that action, only on your explicit click, and can be revoked in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation.

6. System information

Memory and disk usage are read locally to display the status line. These values are shown on screen and never leave your Mac.

7. Children

appGo is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces a feature that needs data processing, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the “Last updated” date will change.
