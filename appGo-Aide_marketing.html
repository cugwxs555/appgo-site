appGo-Aide is a lightweight macOS companion for the few things you actually check every day — and the apps you open every morning.

Everything you glance at, in one place

No dashboards to configure, no widgets to hunt down. Open appGo-Aide and the numbers you care about are already there.

Local weather

Current conditions, next 3 hours and tomorrow, powered by Apple Weather.

  • Feels-like, humidity, wind, pressure
  • UV index and precipitation chance
  • Manual city when you prefer privacy

System status

Memory, disk and CPU load, updated live while the window is open.

  • Used, free and total memory
  • Disk usage at a glance
  • Compact, readable readouts

Quick app launch

Put the apps you open every day in a simple grid, then open or close the whole set with one button.

  • Each card shows “In Use” or “Not Open” in real time
  • One button opens all apps — and switches to “Close All Apps” when any of them is running
  • Built for the start and the end of your working day

Waits quietly at login

Optional launch at login shows a slim bar at the top of the screen. Click it when you want the full window back.

  • “Float on Top” turns the whole window into a top bar
  • Drag the window up to tuck it away, click the bar to restore it
  • Clicking the Dock icon reopens the window too

Made to feel native

A fixed, comfortable dark appearance, real Mac controls, and two languages that follow your system on first launch.

Privacy first

appGo-Aide collects no analytics and does not track you. Weather is requested through Apple WeatherKit, and your city preference stays on your Mac.
